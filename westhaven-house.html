Westhaven House

63 West End Ave, Freeport, NY

Overview

Westhaven House is a transitional shelter in Freeport, NY, offering residents stable housing paired with full-time on-site leadership, case management, and employment and housing support.

  • Location: 63 West End Ave, Freeport, NY
  • Staffing model: Full-time on-site director, plus case manager and social work support
  • Program focus: Case management, employment readiness, permanent housing placement
  • Oversight: Operated in partnership with Nassau County Department of Social Services

Services Provided

On-Site Leadership

A full-time director oversees daily operations, ensuring consistent structure and support for every resident.

Case Management

Individualized service plans guide each resident from intake through permanent housing placement.

Housing Navigation

Residents receive direct support connecting to permanent housing opportunities and benefits coordination.
