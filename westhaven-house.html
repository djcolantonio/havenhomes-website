Westhaven House is a proposed emergency shelter program in Freeport. The service plan combines safe housing with individualized support for adult men working toward stability and permanent housing.

Planned services include case management, licensed social work support, daily meals, transportation coordination, and housing and employment assistance.

These services describe the submitted proposal. Opening and placements remain subject to county approval.

THE PROPOSED PROGRAM

Practical support. Personal goals.

01

A safe place

Structured housing, daily meals, and a team focused on each resident’s stability.

02

Individual support

Case management, benefits assistance, and connections to employment and community services.

03

A way forward

A personal housing plan, practical next steps, and support toward a permanent home.
