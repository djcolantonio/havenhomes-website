About HavenHomes

Our Mission

HavenHomes exists to give individuals and families experiencing homelessness in Nassau County a safe place to stabilize, the support to move forward, and a real path to permanent housing. We believe stable housing is the foundation for everything else — employment, health, and independence.

Our Approach

Each HavenHomes shelter is staffed with dedicated site managers, case managers, and social workers who work directly with residents from intake through move-out. We partner closely with Nassau County Department of Social Services and local organizations to coordinate benefits, employment services, and permanent housing placements.

What We Value

  • Dignity — every resident is treated with respect and given individualized support.
  • Accountability — transparent operations, budgeting, and reporting to our county partners.
  • Forward Movement — every program is designed around a resident's path to permanent housing.
  • Partnership — close collaboration with Nassau County DSS, legislators, and community organizations.
