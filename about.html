OUR APPROACH

Stability starts
with being seen.

A safe place to stay matters. So does having someone help you take the next step.

Local experience.
Personal commitment.

HavenHomes 1 LLC is developing two emergency shelter programs for single adult men in Nassau County. Our plans draw on experience supporting people facing housing instability and recovery across local residential properties.

Each proposed program combines structured housing with case management, social work support, and connections to permanent housing.
