Safe Haven House

38 East Roosevelt Ave, Roosevelt, NY

Overview

Safe Haven House is a transitional shelter in Roosevelt, NY, providing residents with stable housing, individualized case management, and a structured path toward permanent housing and employment.

  • Location: 38 East Roosevelt Ave, Roosevelt, NY
  • Staffing model: Four site manager (4 SM) coverage model, plus case manager and social work support
  • Program focus: Case management, employment readiness, permanent housing placement
  • Oversight: Operated in partnership with Nassau County Department of Social Services

Services Provided

Case Management

Dedicated case managers work with each resident on an individualized service plan from intake through move-out.

Round-the-Clock Coverage

A four-site-manager staffing model ensures consistent, on-site support for residents at all times.

Housing Navigation

Residents receive support connecting to permanent housing opportunities and benefits coordination.
