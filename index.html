How We Support Residents

Every resident works with a dedicated team to move from crisis toward independence.

Case Management

One-on-one case managers and social workers coordinate services, benefits, and next steps for every resident.

Employment Support

Residents are connected to job readiness programs and local employment partners to build financial independence.

Permanent Housing

We work alongside Nassau County partners to move residents from transitional shelter into permanent housing.
